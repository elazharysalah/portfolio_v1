CASE STUDY · AI · AUTOMATION · SOCIAL MEDIA

Building an AI-Powered Content Automation System

An internal system I built to automate content research, analysis, AI generation, captions, and distribution across Facebook and Pinterest.

Role: Software Engineer & System Builder Platforms: Facebook · Pinterest
01 · THE PROBLEM

Too much manual work to maintain content growth

I was managing a home-decoration Facebook page with 50K+ followers, primarily from the United States. Maintaining growth required continuously researching viral content, creating posts, writing captions, scheduling publications, and monitoring performance.

This became a major operational bottleneck, taking time away from my website, brand, and other projects.

02 · THE SOLUTION

Turning content production into an automated system

I built an internal system that analyzed high-performing content, combined those insights with market research, and used AI/LLM APIs to generate new content and captions.

Research → Analyze → AI Generation → Captions → Schedule → Measure

Instead of treating every post as a separate manual task, I created a repeatable content pipeline with performance data feeding back into the process.

03 · PINTEREST

Extending the system to Pinterest

I applied the same approach to Pinterest: identify successful content patterns, generate new content around those patterns, and distribute it toward my website.

100K+ Website users generated through the Pinterest strategy
04 · RESULTS

From manual operations to scalable distribution

50K+ Followers on the original Facebook page
20M+ Views generated for the main brand
5M+ Additional combined views across another brand
+150K Tier-1 website traffic associated with the strategy
05 · LESSONS

What I learned

  • Data can drive creativity: performance data became an input for content generation.
  • AI needs context: combining AI with market research produced a more structured workflow.
  • Automation creates leverage: reducing repetitive work allowed me to focus on strategy and other business activities.
FINAL TAKEAWAY

From manual content creation to an automated content engine.

I built the system to solve a problem I was experiencing myself: spending too much time operating social media instead of building the broader business.

Research. Analyze. Generate. Distribute. Learn. Repeat.
